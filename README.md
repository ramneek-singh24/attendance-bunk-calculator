# Attendance & Bunk Calculator

> **Know your attendance. Plan your classes. Stay on track.**

A clean, responsive single-page web app for university & college students to manage class attendance, calculate safe bunks, and determine recovery requirements.

## 🚀 Live Demo & Features

- **Overall Dashboard**: Real-time radial progress visualization and aggregate statistics across all subjects.
- **Subject-Wise Tracking**: Manage each course individually with quick `+ Attended` and `+ Missed` buttons.
- **Bunk Calculator**: Tells you exactly how many future classes you can skip while staying above your target percentage.
- **Attendance Recovery**: Calculates consecutive classes required to recover if your attendance falls below the target benchmark.
- **Smart Insights**: Automatically highlights at-risk subjects and warnings.
- **Scenario Simulator**: Test custom "what-if" numbers without altering saved subject data.
- **Local Storage**: Automatically saves your subjects and data locally in your browser.
